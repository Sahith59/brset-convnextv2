## Message to Dong

The controlled study has established a strong supervised joint-training baseline and a genuine strict source-only transfer gap. A target-fitted degradation transform made BRSET look closer to mBRSET under five measured appearance averages, but it did not deliver a large diagnostic gain when used in training. Subsequent appearance-error, pruning and simple spatial-pooling screens also failed their prespecified method gates. This is disciplined narrowing, not a demonstrated novel method. We propose an error-led ConvNeXt V2 Large study focused on whether local DR evidence can be stabilized across datasets without degrading ME.

## How to explain the 12 slides

1. **Current answer:** B1 joint training remains the strongest ordinary reference; appearance alone did not solve classification; novelty is open.
2. **Two settings:** In the main setting, mBRSET training labels are available. The separate source-only run has no target exposure before its locked historical assessment. Do not call the main setting zero-shot. The original fit pools differ substantially in label composition: BRSET has 748/11,372 DR-positive images (6.58%) and 274/11,372 ME-positive images (2.41%); mBRSET has 797/3,402 DR-positive images (23.43%) and 291/3,402 ME-positive images (8.55%). At patient level the corresponding DR-positive rates are 437/5,966 (7.32%) versus 276/899 (30.70%); ME-positive rates are 193/5,966 (3.23%) versus 111/899 (12.35%). These counts were recomputed from `step2/full_pool/protocol/full_pool_manifest.csv` using a patient as positive when any of that patient's fit images is positive. They are a potential confounder, not proof of a camera effect.
3. **Steps 0–5:** A six-question history. Each step either established a comparator or ruled out one proposed explanation.
4. **Images Dong requested:** The first four panels in each row share one BRSET training image. The last is an unrelated real mBRSET training image, not paired truth. The rows were traced to raw dataset files and their hashes were checked. Visual resemblance does not prove lesion preservation.
5. **Step 0 and 1:** On a new training-only sample, the frozen historical transform reduced appearance distance from 6.742 to 1.261 under direct 512 resizing and 8.474 to 4.294 under classifier geometry. Step 1 then locked data, labels, model, compute and validation rules.
6. **Step 2:** Across seeds 0–2 on the historically reused 732-image mBRSET test set, B1 joint DR/ME F1 was 0.8338/0.8619 versus B0 target-only 0.8105/0.8240 and B2 sequential 0.8139/0.8184. B1 is a baseline, not a novel method.
7. **Step 3:** Equal-domain sampling raised validation F1 only +0.0035 DR/+0.0079 ME on average and lowered both AUROCs. Keep natural B1.
8. **Step 4:** In a separate training-only refit, full degradation reduced a five-statistic distance from 6.066 to 0.902; nevertheless its classifier DR/ME F1 was 0.8739/0.8485 versus B1 0.8703/0.8489. A1 and A3 also failed the frozen +0.01 F1 gate. The two distance studies have different samples and fit procedures and must not be numerically pooled. Appearance matching succeeded; the proposed diagnostic mechanism failed. A3's DR AUROC +0.0038 is only a small seed-0 validation observation.
9. **World-model premise:** Five global appearance variables added error-prediction AUROC +0.0108 DR and +0.0472 ME, but both patient-bootstrap intervals crossed zero. Positive-case miss AUROCs were 0.555/0.406. CheXWorld is a CVPR radiograph method; these data do not justify investing in its fundus adaptation now.
10. **Step 5:** Strict BRSET-only F1 was 0.7218 DR/0.6972 ME on the historical mBRSET assessment, with sensitivity near 0.60. GFP retained almost every feature and changed neither F1. Top-1 spatial evidence improved DR ranking AUROC +0.0101, but DR F1 −0.0012 and ME AUROC −0.0036; the method gate failed. A high-scoring patch is not confirmed pathology.
11. **Closest methods:** LAT, CCT-Net and Samba already cover lesion-aware or category-specific local evidence; CheXWorld already covers action-conditioned latent prediction. A generic patch head, router or world model is not a defensible novelty claim.
12. **Next experiment and requested decision:** Decide whether the primary paper is supervised joint transfer or source-only transfer; clinically review a stratified sample of false negatives; freeze one ConvNeXt V2 Large mechanism only if the error audit supports it; ablate its components at fixed compute; replicate a validation winner and protect the historical test from further search. Any later result on that test must disclose prior reuse; an independent new cohort would be stronger if available. The internal draft target is 15 October; official ISBI paper submission is 26 October 2026.

**If Dong asks about his earlier shared/private-encoder request:** the older pilot showed a small AUROC increase but lower DR recall/F1, and he suggested a longer 50–100-epoch continuation. We have **not** completed that continuation. The modern B1/C1 training curves cannot settle convergence for a different architecture. A new shared/private run would need its own fixed validation rule, longer-budget B1 control and inference-consistent routing; it is not a current positive or negative Step-5 result. The original visual request is now covered by slide 4, but lesion-level preservation remains unverified. The CheXWorld-style generative/world-model adaptation has been investigated as literature and explicitly deferred after the appearance-error gate; no such model was trained.

## Immediate, falsifiable research path

1. **Error phenotype audit, no GPU:** On the mBRSET validation cohort, stratify B1 misses by DR/ME label, confidence, image quality, simple crop/scale sensitivity and acquisition metadata that actually exist. Use patient units. Select a small stratified image set for Dong's/clinical review, without calling model saliency lesion ground truth.
2. **Freeze a hypothesis only after the audit:** If missed positives genuinely depend on small retinal findings, use the existing ConvNeXt V2 Large and compare label-wise local/global evidence agreement against B1, local-only and agreement-only controls. Keep the same training pool, update budget, validation cohort and two outputs. This is a *candidate experiment*, not an established novel method; LAT/CCT/Samba require a closer overlap analysis before any claim.
3. **Stopping rule:** Require at least +0.01 validation F1 for one label, no worse than −0.01 F1 for the other and no worse than −0.01 AUROC for either. Replicate a passing candidate over seeds 1 and 2, calculate patient-level intervals and assess once after freezing. If the audit does not support local-evidence failure, stop this branch and discuss an empirical transfer/negative-results paper rather than adding modules.
4. **World-model branch:** Reconsider only with a supported camera-linked failure mode and evidence that transformations preserve diagnostic content. Do not infer it from global brightness/blur matching alone.

## Primary conference sources used in the slides

- [Lesion-Aware Transformers for Diabetic Retinopathy Grading, CVPR 2021](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2021/html/Sun_Lesion-Aware_Transformers_for_Diabetic_Retinopathy_Grading_CVPR_2021_paper.html): weakly supervised lesion-region modeling from image-level grades.

- [Samba, NeurIPS 2024](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/hash/8aa0c4d28021c0b273480f9e2aab83a6-Abstract-Conference.html): severity-aware patch sequence modeling for cross-domain grading; source-only grading protocol differs.
- [CheXWorld, CVPR 2025](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2025/html/Yue_CheXWorld_Exploring_Image_World_Modeling_for_Radiograph_Representation_Learning_CVPR_2025_paper.html): parameter-conditioned latent prediction in radiographs, not evidence of benefit for our measured fundus shift.
- [GDRNet / FundusAug, MICCAI 2023](https://conferences.miccai.org/2023/papers/658-Paper1591.html): prior fundus artifact augmentation; our A1 is a component reproduction, not all of GDRNet.
- [ISBI 2027 official important dates](https://biomedicalimaging.org/2027/): four-page paper deadline Monday 26 October 2026.
