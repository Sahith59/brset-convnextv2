## Message to Dong

The controlled study has established a strong supervised joint-training baseline and a genuine strict source-only transfer gap. A target-fitted degradation transform made BRSET look closer to mBRSET under five measured appearance averages, but it did not deliver a large diagnostic gain when used in training. Subsequent appearance-error, pruning and simple spatial-pooling screens also failed their prespecified method gates. This is disciplined narrowing, not a demonstrated novel method. We propose an error-led ConvNeXt V2 Large study focused on whether local DR evidence can be stabilized across datasets without degrading ME.

## How the slide-4 images were made

Each row starts with **one BRSET training photograph**. We resize it bilinearly to 560 × 560 and take a 512 × 512 crop. The additional arm-specific operation is applied to **BRSET training images only**; mBRSET training images skip it. All classifier arms otherwise keep the same B1 fit pool, ordinary flips/rotation/color jitter, ConvNeXt V2 Large, loss, 5,775 updates and validation rules. The rightmost real mBRSET photo is **unpaired**: it is a reference for appearance, not the same eye photographed by a second camera. These statements come from `step4/train_augmentation.py`, `step4/preflight.json` and the raw-file audit.

- **A1, FundusAug artifact component:** our independent 512-pixel implementation of the artifact part of the GDRNet/FundusAug release, **not full GDRNet**. Five operators—sharpness change, bright halo, dark diffuse hole, small bright spots and Gaussian blur—are each sampled with probability 0.5. Their strengths use the published/release ranges, not parameters estimated from mBRSET. Common B1 geometry and color jitter are kept separately. See `step4/step4_transforms.py` and `step4/STEP_4_PLAN_DRAFT.md`.
- **A2, target-fitted full transform:** we measured mean sharpness, brightness, contrast, center-to-edge illumination falloff and saturation on all 3,402 mBRSET **training** images. We searched 256 random parameter candidates plus two starting candidates using 96 BRSET training patients and checked the selected setting on another 96 patient-disjoint BRSET training patients. The objective sums squared differences from mBRSET means, each divided by the mBRSET training standard deviation. It fits appearance **without paired images or diagnosis labels**. The selected chain is blur → uneven illumination → synthetic artifacts → sensor noise → brightness/contrast/saturation gains. Its selected full setting used blur σ = 0.563, illumination strength 0.294, **one bright spot, zero dark holes, halo strength 0.122**, brightness gain 0.862, contrast gain 1.871, saturation gain 0.666 and noise σ = 0.00436. See `step4/refit_degradation.py`, `scripts/55_degradation_ops.py` and `step4/degradation_refit.json`.
- **A3, separately fitted without overlays:** “synthetic overlay” means the code **draws** an artificial bright spot, dark patch or ring-shaped halo on top of the real photograph. A3 forces the counts/strengths of those three additions to zero and **refits all remaining parameters**. It still uses blur, uneven illumination, sensor noise and color changes; it is not simply an undegraded image or A2 with spots erased afterward. The A2 optimum already selected zero dark holes, so the visual distinction here mainly removes its bright spot and halo.

The fitted A2/A3 transforms reduced a five-summary appearance distance from 6.066 (unchanged source) to 0.902/0.942 on held-out BRSET training patients. That is evidence of matching these **averages**, not a paired camera simulator or proof that microaneurysms, exudates and other diagnostic detail survive. The classifier screen did not pass the +0.01 F1 replication gate.

## Why Step 3 and Step 5 are in the story

**Step 3 isolates sampling.** B1 combines 11,372 BRSET and 3,402 mBRSET fit images. Drawing naturally from that pool shows mBRSET about 23% of the time. C1 draws the two datasets equally often, so mBRSET gets 50% of training draws without changing the data available or total optimizer updates. B0, which draws 100% mBRSET, had already scored below B1 on the historical assessment; B1's advantage cannot simply mean it saw *more* target draws than B0. C1 asks the narrower question of whether increasing target frequency *within joint training* helps. It did not give a compelling two-label gain, so later experiments retained B1 as the reference. C2/C3 checked label-mix explanations in seed-0 screens and did not qualify for replication. No Step-3 test assessment was performed.

**Step 5 tests explanations before building another model.** First, the patient-grouped appearance-error analysis tested the premise for a camera-action/world-model branch; it failed its predeclared gate. Second, strict source-only training measured the gap when no mBRSET labels are available. Third, GFP asked whether discarding final feature channels helps. Fourth, a top-1 local score asked whether global averaging hides useful localized signal. The last two used frozen B1 features and validation only, and neither met the DR/ME decision gate. That sequence narrows the next question to the *actual error cases*, not to another untested appearance generator.

## Immediate, falsifiable research path

1. **Error phenotype audit, no GPU:** On the mBRSET validation cohort, stratify B1 misses by DR/ME label, confidence, image quality, simple crop/scale sensitivity and acquisition metadata that actually exist. Use patient units. Select a small stratified image set for Dong's/clinical review, without calling model saliency lesion ground truth.
2. **Freeze a hypothesis only after the audit:** If missed positives genuinely depend on small retinal findings, use the existing ConvNeXt V2 Large and compare label-wise local/global evidence agreement against B1, local-only and agreement-only controls. Keep the same training pool, update budget, validation cohort and two outputs. This is a *candidate experiment*, not an established novel method; LAT/CCT/Samba require a closer overlap analysis before any claim.
3. **Stopping rule:** Require at least +0.01 validation F1 for one label, no worse than −0.01 F1 for the other and no worse than −0.01 AUROC for either. Replicate a passing candidate over seeds 1 and 2, calculate patient-level intervals and assess once after freezing. If the audit does not support local-evidence failure, stop this branch and discuss an empirical transfer/negative-results paper rather than adding modules.
4. **World-model branch:** Reconsider only with a supported camera-linked failure mode and evidence that transformations preserve diagnostic content. Do not infer it from global brightness/blur matching alone.
